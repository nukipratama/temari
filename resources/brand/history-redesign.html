History — mobile redesign (round 3)

Real Pewter tokens (resources/css/app.css). Redesign of resources/js/pages/Activities/Feed.tsx — layout only, no dev work yet. Grounded in the real page: Feed/Calendar are genuinely separate views behind one route, a weekly recap sourced from WeeklySnapshot, and Kartu (RunCard) vs. plain runs — using the real mood taxonomy (resources/js/lib/mood.ts) and rarity tokens (app.css) throughout.

Round 3 — the thread is gone. Grilled this properly before rebuilding: the vertical thread/mascot-node timeline read as decoration bolted onto a list, not something that made runs easier to scan — the actual job of this page is fast, recency-first browsing, not a narrative experience. Rebuilt as a plain, hairline-divided list (closer to how a real activity feed reads) with stats at full visual weight; mood color and Kartu rarity survive as small accents (a dot, a star) instead of leading the row. The journey strip (first-run-vs-latest comparison) is cut entirely — that belongs in a profile/trends context, not blocking the run list. The weekly recap card survives, lighter than before. Header keeps its full italic-display treatment; that wasn't the problem. Every week gets a recap card now, not just the current one — showing only one read as an inconsistency, not a deliberate simplification. Its mascot is bigger and tinted by that week's own dominant mood (same mood-dot tokens the run rows use), instead of a fixed neutral face. Content states are covered too: no runs yet (mascot empty state), this week not started yet (its section is simply omitted, last week still shows), and older history (a "load older weeks" control paginates the Feed further back — Calendar stays a separate, full-browse view rather than absorbing overflow). Filters/sort are cut entirely — not needed on top of range, mood, and Kartu already reading at a glance in this list. Same simplifications as before: Feed only, 365-run cap isn't built here.
