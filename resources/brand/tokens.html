Spacing — new

4px base. Components ask for a role (--pad-card, --pad-panel, --pad-chip) rather than a number, which is what stops twenty-three different paddings appearing across one app.

--pad-chip4px 12px
--pad-panel12px 16px
--pad-card16px
--pad-hero24px
--pad-page48px 40px 80px
