Sportier — same idea, with motion in it

The concept holds: laps, each longer than the last, gold on the most recent. What was missing is motion. Three levers, combined below — lean (forward skew), taper (strokes thin at the tail and thick at the head, so the mark accelerates rather than sits), and drift (centres shift forward so laps trail instead of stacking concentrically). Top row is the current mark for comparison.
