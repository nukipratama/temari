Temari — nested arcs, incumbent evolved

Three laps, each longer than the last. Gold on the outermost arc — the most recent, furthest lap. Indigo on the two inside it: your earlier selves.

Primary · on cream

Gold here is #ae8430, not #d9a53c. Bright gold on cream measures under 2:1 and these strokes are thin — it dissolves. The bright gold is kept for indigo grounds below, where it has the contrast to earn it.

Reversed · on indigo

Small-size variant

Below ~28 px the three-arc mark loses its inner gaps and turns to mush. The small variant drops to two arcs with a heavier stroke. Compare the two rows — the switchover point is around 24 px.

Wordmark — lowercase

The wordmark is lowercase, matching the voice. It also suits this mark: "temari" is almost entirely x-height, with only the t ascender and the i dot breaking the line — it sits level beside a circular mark where capitals would tower over it.
