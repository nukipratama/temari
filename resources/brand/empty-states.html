Empty states

Six, not the three in the brief. An empty state is where a training partner either sounds like a friend or like a 404, so the sentence matters more than the drawing — the art is deliberately faint and the copy carries it. Two rules held throughout: never blame the user, and never apologise for something that is about to fix itself.
