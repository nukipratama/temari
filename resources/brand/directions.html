Six visual directions

Same composition, same generator, same size, six grounds. The first column is the palette that is live today — without it the comparison is dishonest, because a palette can look new in a swatch grid and identical in situ. Porcelain and Pewter are the odd ones out on purpose: both are built to sit under the Luma shadcn component style, so they move shape and type as well as colour. Pewter carries Graphite's palette unchanged on that geometry, so read those two columns as a pair — between them, shape is the only variable.

What the rebrand actually moved so far. Against main, 36 of 43 --color-* tokens are byte-identical. Seven changed, all accents (ember, ember-deep, leaf, leaf-deep, citrus, stone, line); one was dropped (citrus-deep); 16 were added, 15 of them derived -ink accessibility variants plus line-strong. cream, cream-deep, ink, sky, horizon and every surface never moved — the ground, the text tier and the primary surfaces are exactly what they were. That is why every component still feels like before, and it is also why changing it now is cheap: everything is tokenized, so a direction below is an edit to the @theme static block, not a rebuild.

What this page does not decide for you

Held constant on purpose: the semantic accents (leaf, ember, citrus) and the whole rarity ladder are identical in all six columns, so the only variables are ground, structure, accent and type. Retinting those is a separate decision and a separate diff.

Porcelain and Pewter are not like-for-like palette comparisons, and that is deliberate. The other four hold shape constant so the ground is the only variable. These two move radius, elevation and padding too, because pairing with Luma is a geometry decision before it is a colour one, and judging them on a flat scale would be judging something nobody proposed. The consequence is that neither column answers “what if we only changed the colours”. For the inverse reading — what the geometry alone does — compare Pewter against Graphite: their palettes are byte-identical, so shape and type are the only things that differ between them.

The card barely moves, and that is real. Compare the six Kartu: they are nearly indistinguishable in colour and in shape. The frame is sky-deep, the rarity ladder is held, and --r-mount/--r-kartu/--r-art/--r-chip are exempt from the soft scale, so a collectible reads as a collectible whatever the ground does. Decide whether that is the point (the collection is its own world) or a problem (the app's most saturated surface stays on the old identity). If it is a problem, the fix is a separate slice — retinting the rarity ladder — and it is not on this page.

Third-party marks are fixed: the Strava pill keeps #fc4c02 in every direction. It is contractually not ours to theme, and it is worth checking how it sits on each ground — it is the one element that cannot be made to harmonise.

Type stays inside what the app already loads. Every voice here is built from Fraunces (upright and italic), Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono — the three families app.blade.php already requests. No direction needs a new webfont, so none of them carries a licensing question. If a genuinely new face is wanted, that is a research task, not a choice available today.
